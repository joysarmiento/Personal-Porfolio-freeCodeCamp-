Harry Potter: The Golden Trio

The Harry Potter series, written by J.K. Rowling, is one of the most beloved fantasy franchises in history. Comprising seven novels and eight blockbuster films, the series follows Harry Potter, a young wizard, as he learns about magic, forms unbreakable friendships, and confronts the dark wizard Lord Voldemort.

The series has sold over 500 million copies worldwide, been translated into more than 80 languages, and inspired theme parks, merchandise, and a passionate global fanbase. Its themes of love, bravery, and friendship have resonated with generations.

Timeline of Key Events

  • 1997 — Release of Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone (Sorcerer's Stone in the U.S.)
  • 1998 — Release of Harry Potter and the Chamber of Secrets
  • 1999 — Release of Harry Potter and the Prisoner of Azkaban
  • 2000 — Release of Harry Potter and the Goblet of Fire
  • 2001 — First film adaptation premieres
  • 2003 — Release of Harry Potter and the Order of the Phoenix
  • 2005 — Release of Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince
  • 2007 — Release of Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows
  • 2010-2011 — Final book adapted into two films
  • 2016 — Launch of the play Harry Potter and the Cursed Child
